## Figure: policy_compliance_overview

Policy compliance under contextual vetoes ($N=1000$, $K=100$, $1000$ Monte Carlo replications, veto fraction $0.20$). (\textbf{a}) Illustrative synthetic population (trial~0): $R$ vs.\ $Q$ with $Q_i=0$ and high $R_i$. (\textbf{b}) Boxplots of aggregated scores $P_i$ at $\lambda\in\{0.50, 0.75, 0.90\}$; operators are distinguished by colour. (\textbf{c}) Policy violation rate $V(\lambda)$; curves are Monte Carlo means and shaded bands are 95\% percentile intervals.
